DUEVINCI COURSEWORK IMPORTER

How the connection works

Use your existing DueVinci sign-in

This extension does not ask for your DueVinci password or store a second credential. Keep a signed-in duevinci.tech tab open, then use Connect in the popup.

1

Open duevinci.tech and sign in.

2

Open your school’s course page in another tab.

3

Click this extension, scan, review, and import.

What gets written

The extension supports selectable WGU, Maestro, Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, Brightspace, and generic page profiles. It uses DueVinci’s existing Supabase tables: one courses row and selected assignments. Imported rows use lms_provider and lms_source_id so repeated scans upsert instead of duplicating work.

Assignments are created incomplete with a medium priority. Detected quizzes map to exam; other coursework maps to lesson.
